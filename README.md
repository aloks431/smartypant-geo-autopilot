# GEO & SEO Autopilot by Smartypant

Audits, fixes and tracks your AI visibility in ChatGPT, AI search and Google.

Customers increasingly ask AI assistants which business to choose. This plugin measures whether they recommend yours. Smartypant asks ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews the questions your customers ask, and shows you the real results right in the conversation:

- Your AI visibility score (generative engine optimization, GEO) and your SEO health score
- For each customer question: which assistants recommend or cite your business, and which competitors they name instead
- What the assistants actually say about your business
- The competitors AI search cites most in your category
- The most important SEO and AI visibility findings for your website

Each account includes one free check. With a Smartypant plan, Smartypant fixes the issues on your website (WordPress), writes the pages you are missing and re-checks your visibility every week.

Example prompts: "Is my website visible in ChatGPT? Check example.com", "Run an SEO and AI visibility audit of my site", "Show my GEO report", "Which competitors does AI recommend instead of me?"

## Setup

1. Add the plugin, then connect GEO & SEO Autopilot by Smartypant on the plugin's Connectors tab.
2. Log in with your Smartypant account (or create one for free) and click Allow.

## Data

The plugin contains one skill with usage instructions and a reference to the Smartypant connector at https://geo.smartypant.xyz/mcp. When you start a check, Smartypant reads your public website and sends customer-style questions about your business category to the AI assistants listed above to measure the answers. Through the connector, Claude reads the results of your checks (scores, questions, answer excerpts, competitor names and findings). The plugin itself stores nothing and sends data nowhere else.
