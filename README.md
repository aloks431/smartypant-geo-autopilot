# GEO & SEO Autopilot by Smartypant

Audits, fixes and tracks your SEO and AI visibility in Google, ChatGPT and AI search, every week on autopilot.

GEO & SEO Autopilot by Smartypant takes care of how your business is found online: in Google search (SEO) and in AI answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews (AI SEO, also called LLM SEO, answer engine optimization or AEO, and generative engine optimization or GEO). It audits your website, fixes what holds it back, writes and publishes the pages you are missing, and repeats it every week.

**Copilot or autopilot.** Once your website is connected, Smartypant re-checks your SEO and AI visibility every week and works through what it finds: technical and on-page fixes applied to your site, every change reversible, and new articles and pages, fact-checked and optimized for Google and AI answers, published on your website. In copilot mode, you approve each fix and article first. In autopilot mode, Smartypant applies the fixes and publishes the content on its own.

**SEO.** Smartypant analyses the search results for your topics and finds the keywords you do not rank for yet, the ones where you rank but not near the top, and what holds those pages back. It shows the topics your competitors rank for where you have no page. With Google Search Console connected, it uses your real search data. A technical audit covers crawling, indexing, redirects, titles, meta descriptions, headings, alt text, internal links and structured data.

**AI visibility.** Smartypant asks these assistants the questions your customers ask, live, and shows their real answers right in the conversation: who recommends or cites you, your position and sentiment, your share of voice, which competitors are named instead, which sources AI relies on, and whether AI crawlers can read your site.

Example prompts: "Is my business recommended by ChatGPT? Check example.com", "Run an SEO and AI visibility audit of my site", "Show my GEO report", "Which competitors does AI recommend instead of me?"

## Setup

1. Add the plugin, then connect GEO & SEO Autopilot by Smartypant on the plugin's Connectors tab.
2. Log in with your Smartypant account (or create one for free) and click Allow.

## Data

The plugin contains one skill with usage instructions and a reference to the Smartypant connector at https://geo.smartypant.xyz/mcp. When you start a check, Smartypant reads your public website and sends customer-style questions about your business category to the AI assistants listed above to measure the answers. Through the connector, Claude reads the results of your checks (scores, questions, answer excerpts, competitor names and findings). The plugin itself stores nothing and sends data nowhere else.
